Apeing Cultist Simulator's "Principles", let's define a starter pack of magic for Hedge Wizard, and some placeholder tiers to play with.

Instead of 'Principles', we have 'The Ways'. This feels nebulous, flexible, and folkloric.

To define a basic set of cards, we have 4 category sets: Reagents (things spent to do magic), Signs (events that influence magic), Implements (tools that channel magic), and Boons (gifts from the spirits, aid from the beings of magic themselves)

So an example set would be
|The Way of|Reagent|Sign|Implement|Boon|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Augury|tea leaves|flight of birds|dice|glimpse|
|Conjuration|salt|footprints|bell|date of faerie ball|
|Glamour|cicada wing|white dawn|scrap of silk|pixie gossip|

Each of these will have 6 tiers of power for the prototype. The goal of the game (perhaps an Act I sort of thing) will be to achieve a Great Working of Magick, a ritual with the full 24 degrees of power across all 4 categories.

For the prototype, for 2 Ways we'll just have Reagent 1-6, Sign 1-6, Implement 1-6, Boon 1-6.
